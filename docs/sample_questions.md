# Sample Questions

Here are some example prompts you can use with the AI agents in the Video-Agents-Foundry-Solution to explore its video analysis capabilities.

## Video Analysis Prompts

- Summarize the key events in this video and provide timestamps for each
- What objects or people appear in the first 30 seconds of the video?
- Are there any safety violations visible in this video footage?

## Retail Analytics Prompts

- How many customers entered the store in the last hour based on the video feed?
- Which product displays are getting the most customer interaction?
- What is the average queue length at the checkout counters?

## Manufacturing and Quality Control Prompts

- Are there any visible defects on the production line?
- Is every worker on the factory floor wearing the required PPE (hard hat, safety vest, goggles)?
- How many items passed through the assembly line in the last shift?

## Safety and Compliance Prompts

- Are there any unauthorized personnel in the restricted area?
- Detect any hazardous conditions visible in the surveillance feed
- Generate a compliance report for the last 24 hours of safety monitoring

## General Agent Prompts

- What capabilities does the Video Indexer provide for this video?
- Describe the transcribed speech from this video segment
- What faces or known individuals appear in this video?

> **Note:** The specific prompts available depend on the AI agents configured in your deployment and the video sources connected to the Video Indexer Arc Extension.
